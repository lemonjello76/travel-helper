Privacy Policy

Travel Helper · Smooth Rock Media LLC · Last updated September 1, 2026

Travel Helper is built to keep your travel data on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics trackers, and we never sell or share your data with anyone. Here is exactly what the app touches and where it goes.

What the app stores, and where

Trip details (flights, confirmation codes, seats, hotel, rental car, your name as ticketed, addresses you enter) are stored locally on your device. If cloud sync is enabled, the trip file is also stored in the app's cloud database so your other devices can load it. It is used only to run your trip — nothing else.

Location (GPS) is used on your device, in the moment, to advance trip phases (arriving at the airport, landing at a connection) and estimate drive times. Your location is never uploaded, logged, or stored anywhere. Location is only read while the app is open, and only after you grant permission.

Notifications are generated on your device for phase changes and boarding reminders. No notification content leaves your device.

AI features

When you use the optional AI features — filling the trip form from a confirmation email or screenshot, building an airport playbook, or refreshing a Plan B ladder — the content you provide (the pasted email text or the screenshot image) is sent to Anthropic's API to be read, and the answer comes back to your device. That content is processed to answer your request and is subject to Anthropic's privacy policy. We do not run a server in the middle and we never see it.

Weather

Forecasts come from the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov) using the airport's published coordinates — not your personal location.

Third-party links

Buttons that open Waze, Uber, Lyft, airline apps, FlightAware, Google Flights, and similar services hand you off to those services. What happens there is governed by their privacy policies.

Data deletion

Use CLEAR TRIP in the app, or uninstall the app, to remove your data from the device. If cloud sync was enabled, clearing the trip removes the synced copy on next sync.

Children

Travel Helper is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions: Smooth Rock Media LLC — via the support link on the app's store listing.
