Why this exists

Built by a guy who flies for a living — and got burned twice in one weekend.

Delayed four hours after boarding. Taxied to the runway, taxied back, deplaned. The connection was dead before takeoff — and 140 people stood up to fight for one gate agent.

The traveler who reaches the agent first — with a backup route already in hand — flies home. Everyone else gets a hotel voucher. If they're lucky.

Flight Plan B is that first traveler, automated: it knows your seat, your walking pace, your airport's habits, the weather in your exact connection window — and it keeps a ladder of backup options loaded before you ever board.
